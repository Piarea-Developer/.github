# PIAREA Developer

Welcome to the official developer workspace for **PIAREA**.

PIAREA is an environment, technology, and creative-service company focused on helping public and private sector partners build practical, sustainable, and data-driven solutions. Our work covers environmental consulting, environmental risk assessment, environmental management, spatial mapping, digital platforms, education technology, and internal business systems.

This GitHub organization is used to manage source code, technical documentation, deployment workflows, and engineering standards for PIAREA digital products and services.

---

## About PIAREA

PIAREA provides integrated services and applicative digital products for various partners, including government institutions, educational institutions, and private companies.

Our focus areas include:

- Environmental risk assessment
- Environmental management
- Spatial mapping and geospatial systems
- Digital platform development
- Education and training platforms
- Survey and data management systems
- Digital libraries and knowledge-sharing platforms
- Creative and technology-based service delivery
